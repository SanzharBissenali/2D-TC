Learned vs gated sign head — 3×3 field grid

Doubled semion, 2×3 honeycomb (27 qubits), hx ∈ {0.4, 0.8, 1.2} × hz ∈ {0, 0.2, 0.4}. Arms share trunk, optimizer, sampler and the 350-step budget. M: ψ = A·tanh(MLP(ε,x)), sign learned from the energy. M-pre: same, MLP pre-fit to the ED signs at that point. T: ψ = as·Atriv + shead·Atop, deterministic head, no learned sign. Notebook: analysis/07_signbench_heatmaps.ipynb (branch learned-sign-head).

The three architectures

All arms share the positive Combo trunk A(σ) > 0 (local CNN → Wilson vertex tokens Qv → all-to-all invariant CNN → exp), the Phase-3 recipe (minSR, lr 0.01, diag-shift 6e-5, 8192 samples, 350 steps, seed 0) and one deterministic feature map σ → (ε, x): read the Qv syndrome, pair defects with MWPM → recovery ε, repair r = σ ⊕ ε (closed-loop sector), solve x = G−1r over GF(2) — xp ∈ {0,1} marks which hexagons were flipped from all-up to reach r. (ε, x) determines σ, so every arm sees the full configuration in decoder coordinates.
